COIL
15486372

Firing order

1 → 5 → 4 → 8 → 6 → 3 → 7 → 2

Start at the verified #1 tower and follow rotor rotation. The center tower is the coil.

Clean installation

  1. Install rotor and seat the distributor cap.
  2. Verify the physical #1 cap terminal.
  3. Connect cap towers in firing order: 1, 5, 4, 8, 6, 3, 7, 2.
  4. Connect the coil lead to the center tower.
  5. Route 1–2–3–4 together toward the passenger/right bank.
  6. Route 5–6–7–8 together toward the driver/left bank.
  7. Passenger/right bank, front to rear: 1, 2, 3, 4.
  8. Driver/left bank, front to rear: 5, 6, 7, 8.
  9. Clip wires into separators; keep them clear of exhaust and moving parts.
  10. Verify both ends before starting the engine.
Do not guess the #1 tower. If distributor position is uncertain, establish cylinder #1 at compression TDC first.
