M117 firing order

1 → 5 → 4 → 8 → 6 → 3 → 7 → 2

Verify the physical #1 tower before wiring. Center tower = coil.

Cylinder layout

Front of car → firewall
Passenger/right bank: 1 · 2 · 3 · 4
Driver/left bank: 5 · 6 · 7 · 8

Distributor wire installation

  1. Key off. Install rotor and seat/clip distributor cap.
  2. Establish and verify the physical #1 cap tower.
  3. Connect towers in firing order: 1, 5, 4, 8, 6, 3, 7, 2.
  4. Connect coil lead to center tower.
  5. Route 1–2–3–4 together to passenger/right bank.
  6. Route 5–6–7–8 together to driver/left bank.
  7. Connect each bank front-to-rear in numerical order.
  8. Use separators; avoid exhaust, belts, fan and sharp edges.
  9. Verify every lead at both ends before starting.
Do not guess timing, dwell, pressure or adjustment values. Confirm specifications for the exact engine/distributor variant before adjustment.
